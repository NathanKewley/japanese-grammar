How your data is stored (Backup/Restore)

There are no accounts and no server. Everything you do here is saved only in this browser, on this device:

  • The grammar library keeps your saved points and settings in the browser's local storage.
  • The study apps keep lessons passed, review schedules, answers and stats in the browser's IndexedDB storage.
  • A shared record of what you know (the words, kanji, kana and forms you've met or are learning, and the words you've looked up) sits beside them, so every app sees the same picture.

This means:

  • Nothing is sent anywhere, and nobody else can see it.
  • Your progress doesn't follow you to another browser, another device, or a private window.
  • Clearing this site's data (or "cookies and site data") in your browser settings deletes your progress. Some browsers may also clear it if the site goes unused for a long time.

On iPhone and iPad: Safari clears the data of websites you haven't opened for a week. To keep your progress, add this site to your Home Screen (tap Share, then Add to Home Screen) and always open it from there. Home Screen apps aren't cleared like that. The Home Screen app keeps its own progress, separate from Safari's, so pick one and stick with it.

On Android: Chrome keeps your progress unless you clear the site's data or the phone runs very low on space. Installing the site (Chrome menu, then Add to Home screen or Install app) makes it open like an app and helps keep its data safe.

To keep it safe, save a backup file now and then. You can restore it here or in another browser to carry on where you left off. Each app's Settings tab can also back up just that app.

Moving from the Android apps: a backup from the Android Writing, Conjugation, Reading or Listening app restores here too. Use the button below, or that app's Settings tab. It brings over lessons passed, reviews, stats and your study log.
